Request Information

The following is the list of information and supporting documentation needed by Starlinks Legal to initiate legal proceedings:

  1. Brief of the dispute
  2. Signed contract by both parties
  3. All signed appendices by both parties for any services provided
  4. Claimed amount and its detailed break-up
  5. Communication: All correspondence between SL and the client, including email attachments
  6. Responses from the client regarding non-payment, including claims of breach, loss, or damage (emails, letters, or any provided format)
  7. Statements of Account showing the outstanding balance claimed by SL
  8. Copies of all invoices issued to the client
  9. Correspondence where SL requested payment for invoices (including multiple follow-ups if applicable)
  10. Copies of all notices exchanged between parties (suspension, termination, payment requests, etc.) with proof of delivery
  11. Additional documents such as Proof of Delivery, payment records, internal memos, client history, third-party notices, compliance records, and insurance documents
  12. Corporate documents: CR copy and national address (if available)
  13. Contact details of the relevant person to whom the dispute should be served

Please note that all tangible evidence is required to properly prepare for both the claim and any counterclaim. This includes emails, letters, text messages, recorded calls, and any other relevant materials.

If the required information is not directly available, please coordinate with the appropriate team members to obtain it.

Starlinks Legal will not proceed until all required information and documents are provided. No follow-ups will be made if the information is not collated in a timely manner.
